Code of Conduct in Action

Today you're the steward on Gate 3. Work through six short modules — learn the standard, then make the call in a real match-day moment. About 10 minutes, built for your phone.

What you'll be able to do
  • 01Sign on accredited and equipped before gates open.
  • 02Send Control a clear radio call, first time.
  • 03Turn a frustrated spectator around, calmly.
  • 04Spot hazards and escalate the deadliest first.
  • 05Run an incident by the book — facts only.
  • 06Protect the JAM brand — phone away.
Measurable objectives (trainer / LMS)
  1. Given an assigned shift, arrive with accreditation, a working radio and water at least 15 minutes before start, with 100% of essential kit present.
  2. When Control requests an update, transmit a message with callsign, situation, a figure, the trend and "Over" — in order, with no vague or opinion content.
  3. When a spectator is frustrated, de-escalate to a satisfactory resolution by acknowledging, directing specifically, and closing courteously.
  4. Given a crowd scene, identify all hazards (8/8), classify each by urgency, and escalate the highest life-safety risk with a clear call.
  5. After an incident, sequence the six response steps correctly and remove 100% of opinion lines from the report.
  6. On shift, choose not to capture or post, and classify information as report vs never-post with 100% accuracy.
